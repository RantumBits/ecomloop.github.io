the Shopify Tracking & Profit Audit

find out what your tracking is getting wrong before you spend another dollar on ads against it

a fixed-price audit, not a pitch for a retainer

Most stores have a gap between what their analytics report and what Shopify shows in actual orders: a duplicate Purchase event, a Meta CAPI send with no de-duplication against the browser pixel, a Google Ads tag left over from an old account. Every one of those quietly inflates or deflates the numbers a platform's Smart Bidding optimizes toward, and the ROAS a founder reads on a dashboard.

The audit finds exactly what's wrong, tells you what it's costing you, and hands you the 1 or 2 tests your traffic can actually decide in the next 30 days — with the math behind each one. No redesign, no retainer required to see it.

  • Purchase events reconciled across GA4, Meta pixel, Meta CAPI and Google Ads against your real Shopify order data — the overcounting or undercounting found, named exactly
  • Every script and app on your top pages, with the page-speed cost of each one and what's safe to cut
  • The 1 or 2 A/B tests your current traffic can reach significance on within 30 days, with the sample-size math shown

🔎 what this usually finds

example, from a recent teardown

A product page was firing two separate Google Ads conversion tags — an old account still counting next to the current one. Every purchase on that page was being reported twice to Google Ads, which meant Smart Bidding was optimizing against a conversion rate that was never real. Confirmed live in about ten seconds with a tag inspector; the fix took an afternoon.
