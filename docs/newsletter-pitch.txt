Pre-BFCM data: 1 in 6 Shopify storefronts in a random sample send GA4 to two properties

Hi Paul,

I scanned a random sample of Shopify storefronts in the Majestic Million, 386 stores, for tracking faults. On one page load:

- 64 (17%) send data to two or more GA4 properties
- 23 (6%) still load Universal Analytics, which Google shut off in 2023
- 73 (19%) fire the GA4 tag twice
- 31 (8%) fire the Meta pixel twice

A hand-picked panel of 192 DTC brands matched the first two within a point; duplicate firing ran five points higher there.

None of it shows as an error, so merchants go into Black Friday trusting the numbers. Dead UA is safe to remove now; a doubled pixel is one to log and pull after Cyber Monday.
