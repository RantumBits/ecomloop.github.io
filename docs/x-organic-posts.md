**1. Study is out** (266)

> I scanned 386 random Shopify storefronts in October and wrote up what their
> tracking does.
>
> 38% had at least one of three problems: two GA4 properties, Universal
> Analytics still loading, or a tag firing twice on one page.
>
> Numbers and method: https://ecomloop.com/shopify-tracking-study.html

**2. Which tag doubles** (230)

> Of the 105 stores in my sample that fire a tag twice on one page, the tag was
> GA4 in 73. The Meta pixel in 31, Bing in 22.
>
> One common way it happens: the tag is installed through an app and again by
> hand.
>
> https://ecomloop.com/shopify-tracking-study.html

**3. Two GA4 properties, with the caveat** (260, boost candidate)

> 1 in 6 of the Shopify stores I scanned send every visit and sale to two GA4
> properties.
>
> Sometimes that's deliberate, and a scan can't tell. But if nobody on your
> team knows why the second one is there, find out before BFCM.
>
> Free scan: https://scan.ecomloop.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=study-posts&utm_content=two-ga4-post

**4. Dead Universal Analytics** (263, boost candidate)

> Google stopped processing Universal Analytics in July 2023. 23 of the 386
> Shopify stores I scanned still load it on every page view.
>
> If you moved to GA4 and never took the old tag out, it's probably still in
> your theme or GTM.
>
> Free scan: https://scan.ecomloop.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=study-posts&utm_content=dead-ua-post

**5. Method caveat** (266)

> A caveat on my Shopify tracking study: 39 of the 386 stores only let the
> scanner reach the home page, not product, cart or checkout.
>
> So the rates are floors. The real share with a doubled tag or a second GA4
> property is likely a bit higher.
>
> https://ecomloop.com/shopify-tracking-study.html

**6. A question** (213)

> Question for Shopify operators: before BFCM, does your GA4 revenue come out
> higher or lower than Shopify's?
>
> Higher usually means something is counted twice. I'm trying to learn how

## Boosting

Post first, boost later. Each post goes out organically, and after a day or
two the best ones get promoted from X Ads as an existing post, so the ad keeps
its replies and likes.

- **Boost 3 and 4.** They link straight to the scanner with their own
  `utm_content`, so the scan log counts their scans. The tags use
  `utm_medium=social`, so organic and boosted clicks share a tag; the X Ads
  dashboard gives the paid side of the clicks.
- **Boost 6 only for replies.** It has no link. A reply from an operator is a
  conversation, which is worth more than a click, but judge it on replies, not
  scans.
- **Don't boost 1, 2 or 5.** They link to the study page, whose scanner links
  carry no tag, so a scan that came through them looks like any other visit
  from ecomloop.com.
- **How:** a separate campaign (`study-boosts`), objective Website traffic,
  same targeting as the bfcm-tracking ad groups, $10/day per post for 3 days.
  Keep the post with the better cost per scan for the rest of the week and
  stop the other. About $60 for the first 3 days, on top of the $380.
- **Choosing:** @ecomloop has few followers, so organic numbers will be too
  small to pick from. The 3-day paid run is what picks the winner.
- Spend changes in X Ads have to come from Andrew in the campaign session; it
  won't take budget changes relayed from another session.
