# Newsletter candidates for the corpus-403 exclusive

Drafted 2026-10-19. Shopifreaks gets the first look (decided 2026-10-19); the pitch is a first-look offer, so only one at a time. Shopifreaks checked live 2026-10-19; the other four are not checked.

1. **Shopifreaks** (Paul Drecksler, who writes and edits it alone). Weekly roundup of ecommerce news, 20,000+ subscribers including people at Shopify, merchants and app developers. Submit through the form at shopifreaks.com/contact, subject "News Article Submission" (no public email; Cloudflare hides it). Subscribers can also reply to any issue with news, per the site. A Shopify-only data story fits its beat exactly, and a one-person newsletter can say yes quickly.
2. **2PM** (Web Smith). DTC strategy for operators and investors, and it already runs data-heavy analysis. The replication result is the kind of finding it covers.
3. **DTC Newsletter** (Blaine Bolus). Large founder and marketer readership at Shopify brands, written as short practical takeaways. The "count your G- IDs" check fits its format.
4. **eCommerceFuel** (Andrew Youderian). Independent store owners doing roughly $250k and up. The random sample leans toward established stores rather than household brands, which is closer to this audience than to the big-brand panel.
5. **Analytics Mania** (Julius Fedorovicius). GA4 and GTM practitioners, not founders. Worth it as a referral channel: these are the agencies and freelancers who see the symptoms and get hired to fix them.

My order: Shopifreaks first (closest fit, smallest ask), then 2PM.
